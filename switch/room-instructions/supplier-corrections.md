This group is where the buyer's operations team asks for corrections to shipping documents.

Each request names a case code (for example SHP-0247), the field in question, what the invoice
says, what the packing list says, and the page. Reply in that thread in your own words, and
attach the corrected PDF there when you have it. You do not need to mention anyone: replies in
the thread reach the team's assistant automatically.

Please send the document your company issued. Files are checked automatically when they arrive;
Telegram limits files to 20 MB, so for anything larger the assistant will share a secure upload
link.
