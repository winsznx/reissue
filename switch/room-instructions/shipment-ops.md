This is the shipment operations room. To check a shipment, attach the commercial invoice and the
packing list (PDF, scan or XLSX) in one message and mention @reissue. Reissue replies in that
message's thread with what arrived, what differed (with page and row), who is acting and what
changed.

Reply in a case thread to talk about that case. Useful replies:
- `@reissue 1 carton = 20 pcs for LL-SHD-090, source: supplier spec sheet 2026-10-30` records a
  sourced pack size and rechecks.
- `@reissue recheck` reprocesses the current documents.
- `@reissue assign to Dana` changes the case owner.
- `@reissue status` shows open discrepancies and who owes the next step.

@reissue-examiner verifies evidence when the coordinator asks; @reissue-liaison talks to the
supplier. "Ready for broker review" means the current documents passed every internal consistency
check. It is not a customs decision.
